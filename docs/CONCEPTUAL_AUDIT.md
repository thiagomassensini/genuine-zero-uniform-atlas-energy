# Conceptual Audit

## What the formalization says

The native camera and Genuine chart use the same positional wave. Lean checks
their equality at every finite cutoff after the injective real-to-complex
coordinate packaging. Their limit zero predicates are therefore equivalent.

The native quadratic amplitude is fixed before the camera is evaluated:

```math
\left\lVert n^{-1/2}
\bigl(\cos(-t\log n),\sin(-t\log n)\bigr)
\right\rVert^2
=n^{-1}.
```

No theorem in this repository obtains `1/2` from a zero or inserts an
additional condition into the definition of zero.

## Single-system API

The public API has one finite computation, one limiting vanishing predicate,
and two coordinate presentations related by an exact theorem. The real pair

## Meaning of the transverse formulas

Upstream modules define families with a variable radial coordinate because
they are useful for measuring departure from the native shell. This repository
uses that coordinate only through the already-proved radial factorization,
evaluated at the native parameter.

The native theorem is the specialization of that diagnostic formula to the
quadratic positional amplitude.

## What remains after the boundary telescopes

The finite center is formed from the independently defined total coupled Green
flux and signed boundary. Its exact factorization shows that the only surviving
radial factor is the carry displacement from the quadratic shell. Strict
positivity of the remaining pairing prevents accidental cancellation.

Consequently the center, carry tilt, and quadratic branch defect have exactly
the same zero locus under the stated Green-camera hypotheses. At the common
native/Genuine zero, telescoping removes the boundary; it does not manufacture
the balance. The native amplitude has already fixed the radial coordinate at
`1/2`.
