# Theorem Registry

Release `0.10.0` contains 148 ordered public theorem reports. The locked
`0.6.0` base surface remains in
[`theorem-registry.json`](theorem-registry.json). The current cumulative
extension is
[`theorem-registry-0.10.0.json`](theorem-registry-0.10.0.json); the historical
`0.7.0`, `0.8.0`, and `0.9.0` extensions are retained for provenance and are
not renumbered.

The combined registry is checked against declarations in all public modules,
including `NativeCutoffGlobalRemainder.lean`,
`EmpiricalCollectiveEnergyAsymptotic.lean`, `NativeCutoffLogJet.lean`,
`NativeCutoffDifferentiatedRemainder.lean`, and
`NativeCutoffExactScaledTailCauchy.lean`; the ordered `#print axioms` commands
in `Audit.lean`; and complete claim-ledger coverage.

The `0.10.0` extension retains the nine capstones accumulated through `0.9.0`
and adds four reports for the exact scaled-tail analytic layer: the value,
first-derivative, and second-derivative bounds, plus one theorem packaging all
three estimates. On the critical line the common rate is `O(1/M)`; the first
and second derivative constants are generated by Cauchy's estimate on the
fixed radius-`1/4` circle.

The empirical cutoff-doubling campaign is discovery and reproducibility
provenance only. CI does not use its floating-point results as theorem
premises or as a publication gate. The remaining frontier concerns the
concrete moving minimizer and cutoff-uniform global coercivity, not the three
analytic exact-tail inputs.
